# ADR-0004: Reporting and publishing direction

- **Status:** Accepted (2026-07). Tier 1 is built; tiers 2 and 3 are directions, not built.

## Context

The platform needs in-app reports and controlled exports. SharePoint and Power BI are possible consumers, and a future interschool scope includes a public fixtures and results site. None of these consumers is committed, and every integration adds credentials and governance surface a school must manage.

## Decision

Three tiers, with no external integration yet.

1. **In-app reports and CSV exports** (built). Needed under any future choice, and the files are the universal adapter: they can be placed in SharePoint or loaded into Power BI by hand.
   - **No PDF library.** A PDF is the report screen, a print stylesheet and the browser's print dialog. Adding a server-side renderer needs a new ADR.
   - **CSV is generated by the server, for carnival administrators only, and audited.** The whole `/reports` group needs `carnivals:administer`: a file of student names leaving the platform is a staff-desk act. Each CSV writes a `report.export` audit event; viewing the screen is not audited. The live standings read stays on `carnivals:view`.
   - **Reports are an online path.** They are computed on read from everything every device has synced, so a single device's cache has nothing honest to build one from. They are never queued or cached on the device.
   - **Reports work in any carnival status.** A Draft carnival's book is empty rather than refused; a finalised carnival's reports stay readable. The district selection export is the exception: finalised carnivals only ([ADR-0028](0028-records-and-district-selection.md)).
   - **Spreadsheet formula injection is neutralised**: a field beginning with a formula character is prefixed so a spreadsheet reads it as text, unless the whole field is a number.
2. **A read-only venue publishing page** served by the platform: live house scores and results for a screen or projector, non-sensitive data only, names shown no more than would be read over a PA. It would be the seed of the interschool public site, built on one publishing boundary.
3. **Automated SharePoint or Power BI integration**: deferred until the pilot shows real demand.

How reports work: [reports and exports](../architecture/reports.md).

## Consequences

- Report generation stays separate from the transactional core.
- The venue page, when built, needs a "publishable" classification per report, designed once and reused by a public site.
- No external credentials or service principals to manage before production.
- Reports are built in memory; a carnival is hundreds of rows. Outgrowing that is a decision, not a quiet refactor.

## Rejected alternatives

- **A server-side PDF renderer**: a second layout engine and a native binary or headless browser in the API container, for a file that can drift from the screen.
- **Auditing on-screen report views**: buries the exports that matter in noise.
